# Site Art

Create original mid-century editorial illustrations using the specification below. Style-reference images are unnecessary.

## Style

- **Medium:** matte reduction print combined with cut-paper collage on fine uncoated stock. Handmade, considered, and warm.
- **Shapes:** broad confident silhouettes, overlapping angular planes, slightly irregular hand-cut contours. Use two or three flat tonal areas per surface and sparse carved cream highlight lines. Establish clear silhouettes before adding texture.
- **Palette:** warm paper `#F3F1E8`, carbon `#22251F`, terracotta `#A3372D`, muted olive `#596046`, and darker olive `#4C533E`. Cream provides breathing room; carbon anchors; olive supports; terracotta concentrates attention on the focal action. Allow restrained brown, ochre, and natural skin tones for people.
- **Texture:** fine paper fibers, tiny cream speckles within pigment, and subtly uneven dry ink coverage. Keep grain delicate and consistent across assets; it recedes at thumbnail size. Slight edge misregistration is acceptable. No coarse noise overlay or aged distress.
- **Depth:** shallow physical overlap and small flattened contact shadows. Model volume with discrete tonal shapes rather than smooth shading.
- **Composition:** quiet asymmetry, generous negative space, a few large forms, and one clear gesture or conceptual relationship. Supporting objects remain subordinate. Use physical metaphors suited to the subject; foliage is optional.
- **Mood:** thoughtful, human, tactile, and calm.

## Adaptation

- **Editorial scenes:** overhead still life, normally 3:2, with warm paper filling the canvas and no border.
- **Portraits:** complete silhouettes with generous transparent padding and genuine alpha in open gaps. Clothing uses broad graphic planes; faces may retain extra nuance for likeness. Grounding and contact shadows stay modest.
- **Other assets:** adapt the framing and background while preserving the palette, shape simplification, and grain density.

Avoid glossy highlights, 3D rendering, airbrushed gradients, dramatic lighting, glow, neon, photographic patches, mechanical vector perfection, clutter, generic AI brains/robots/circuits, and unrequested lettering, logos, or watermarks.

Check for clear silhouettes, restrained pigments, delicate dry ink, shallow depth, and one readable focal idea at full and thumbnail size. Revise only the mismatch. Preserve unaffected details during edits; explicit user changes override these defaults.
